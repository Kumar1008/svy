What is Moola Dhara Chakra

  • 22nd January 2026

The Moola Dhara Chakra, also known as the Root Chakra, is the first and foundational energy centre of the astral (subtle) body. The word Moola means root or foundation, and Dhara means support. As the name itself suggests, this chakra is the base upon which the entire energetic, physical, emotional, and spiritual structure of a human being rests.

It is located at the base of the spine and governs survival, stability, physical existence, instinct, and the sense of belonging in the material world. Scientifically speaking, it corresponds closely with the pelvic nerve plexus, adrenal system, and the most primal brain responses related to safety and continuity of life.

The Moola Dhara as an Ancestral Energy Centre

One of the most powerful and lesser-understood aspects of the Moola Dhara Chakra is its role as an ancestral storage centre.

This chakra is energetically connected to seven generations of our ancestors—both maternal and paternal. To be precise, 126 individuals belonging to these seven generations are energetically linked and stored within the Moola Dhara Chakra.

What does “stored” mean here?

It means:

  • Their genetic imprints
  • Their unresolved emotional patterns
  • Their belief systems
  • Their survival responses
  • Their collective karmic residue

are carried forward as subtle energy information within this chakra.

From a scientific lens, this aligns with epigenetics, which shows that trauma, fear responses, and survival patterns can be inherited across generations. Spiritually, the Moola Dhara is the meeting point where ancestral memory and individual life force merge.

Storehouse of Past-Life Karmas

The Moola Dhara Chakra is also the primary storage centre for karmas of all previous births. Every action, intention, and unresolved experience from past lives leaves an energetic imprint. These imprints do not float randomly; they are anchored in the root chakra, because karma is directly related to:

  • Survival
  • Physical incarnation
  • Family lineage
  • Birth circumstances
  • Repeated life patterns

This is why the circumstances of the present birth—such as:

  • Family environment
  • Financial conditions
  • Health tendencies
  • Repeating struggles or strengths

are not accidental. They are deeply connected to: The karmic inputs of our own previous births, and The collective karmic load of our seven-generation ancestral line.

In simple terms: Your current life is a continuation, not a beginning.

Why This Chakra Is So Powerful

Because the Moola Dhara Chakra holds:

  • Ancestral karmas (seven generations)
  • Individual past-life karmas
  • Survival instincts
  • Physical incarnation energy

it becomes the most powerful and sensitive chakra in the human energy system.

If this chakra is blocked or imbalanced, individuals may experience:

  • Chronic fear or insecurity
  • Financial instability
  • Repeating family patterns
  • Health issues related to the lower body
  • A constant feeling of being “unsupported” in life

When it is balanced and purified, a person experiences:

  • Deep inner stability
  • Strong grounding and confidence
  • Healing of ancestral patterns
  • Liberation from repetitive karmic cycles
  • A solid foundation for higher spiritual growth

In Essence

The Moola Dhara Chakra is not just a survival centre. It is:

  • The root of incarnation
  • The archive of ancestral memory
  • The vault of past-life karmas
  • The bridge between lineage, karma, and present life

Working consciously with this chakra means healing not only oneself, but the past and the future of an entire lineage.
