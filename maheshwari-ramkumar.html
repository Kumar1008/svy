A Beautiful Transformation with Sittha Viruthi Yoga

  • 27th January 2026
Thank you to everyone

May many more souls experience this light, healing, and transformation Thank you to everyone who made this sacred space possible

Beyond physical healing, this path opened my awareness to deeper truths. I understood how our ancestors play a vital role in our lives, how we are connected to them through our DNA, and I began offering prayers of gratitude with deep reverence. I also realized the true power of forgiveness, which brought immense emotional freedom and inner peace.

My attitude towards life, family members, and others has completely transformed. I now approach situations with understanding, compassion, and clarity, and I’m able to handle life more gracefully. Every technique taught helped me shift to a positive outlook, and my consistent practice of these techniques continues to support and uplift me every single day.

This journey has felt like a divine awakening—enriching, uplifting, and deeply soulful. Each session unfolds higher truths, explained with scientific clarity and practical examples, making them easy to understand and apply in daily life. The doubts and fears I once carried, especially around rituals, have dissolved, replaced by clarity and confidence.

This journey with Sittha Viruthi Yoga is truly a treasure—every teaching a priceless gem that I hold with gratitude and reverence.
