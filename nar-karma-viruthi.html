What is – Narkarma Viruthi?

A one-day, powerful spiritual workshop to reset health and awaken inner clarity.

This first level places maximum emphasis on Health, the foundation upon which the richness of wealth, loving relationships, and mental wellbeing can be experienced. Participants will learn essential lifestyle principles designed to improve physical and emotional health. Structure of the Workshop

The one-day workshop is delivered in two profound sessions:

Session 1 – Spiritual Science

This session introduces the participants to the spiritual science of life and evolution based on the teachings of Vethathiri Maharishi.

Topics include:

  • The nature of the Infinite Empty Space — omnipresent, omniscient, and omnipotent
  • The formation of atomic particles, the universe, planets and the five elements
  • The emergence of the sixth sense and the birth of humanity
  • The causes and challenges of human suffering — how, why and when they arise
  • Timeless solutions drawn from ancient Indian wisdom: Bhagavad Gita, Vedas, and the teachings of the Siddhars

This session provides profound clarity on the origins of life, the nature of the mind, and the reasons behind human struggles.

Session 2 – Spiritual & Meditative Techniques

Participants are initiated into six powerful spiritual solutions designed to bring positive energy, cleanse karmic patterns, and lead one into a life of inner peace.

Three Techniques to Bring in Positive Energy

  • Meditation – quietens the mind and awakens inner stillness
  • Manifestation – aligning thought and energy to create positive outcomes
  • Awareness – staying present to prevent negative thoughts and emotions

Three Techniques to Cleanse Negative Karma

  • Thithi – prayers and offerings for ancestral purification
  • Forgiveness – a deep, guided process to release emotional burdens
  • Chakra Cleansing – clearing emotional blocks through breath and mudras

Each technique is taught with clarity, with ongoing follow-ups to support consistent practice.

The Role of Meditation

Participants receive a short introductory meditation to experience inner calm. Meditation is the doorway to merging with the Higher Existence. But this deep state requires a mind free from emotional impurities. As cleansing takes place, manifestation becomes faster, and the heart and soul align with the Ultimate Divine Energy.

The Journey Forward

Regular practice of these six powerful solutions naturally improves both health and mental well-being. Nar Karma Viruthi becomes the gateway to expanded self-knowledge, preparing participants for the next levels of Sittha Viruthi Yoga. It is the first step toward a life filled with love, peace and joy — within oneself, at home, and in the world.
