I am Sreevidhya Maheshkumar from Coimbatore.

  • 3rd March 2026

After joining Sittha Viruthi Yoga, my journey toward a blissful state truly began. Through disciplined practice, proper guidance, and sincere involvement, I experienced gradual yet powerful changes within myself. Within six months, my health showed clear improvement, and my medical reports reflected positive changes.

I could feel strength, clarity, and balance returning to my body, mind, and soul.

Through understanding the removal of karma, the soulful practice of Thithi for ancestors, and the path of forgiveness, I realized the true value of life. These practices opened a powerful path of inner cleansing and spiritual transformation.

My journey toward a blissful state continues. With deep gratitude and devotion, I remain blessed with good health, clarity, and spiritual growth.
