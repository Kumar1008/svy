Sittha Viruthi Yoga
Ancient Siddhar Science for a Healthy, Peaceful & Prosperous Life

Sittha Viruthi Yoga is a unique, insight-driven yogic science designed to transform life at its deepest level—the Energy Body, where all experiences, emotions, and karma are stored. Rooted in the timeless wisdom of Indian ancient scriptures and the mystical knowledge of the Siddhars (Sitthars), this system offers practical and powerful one-day workshops that help individuals overcome struggles of Health, Mind, Relationships, and Wealth.

At the heart of this system lies a simple but profound truth:
Karma shapes life. Cleansing Karma reshapes destiny.

Through structured guidance, transformative meditations, and energy-body practices, Sittha Viruthi Yoga helps you release negative karmic patterns, strengthen your chakras, and open the path to a life of love, peace, health, prosperity, and fulfillment.

Why Sittha Viruthi Yoga?

A Practical, Time-Honored Science to Heal the 4 Dimensions of Life

Every human challenge—whether it manifests as illness, emotional disturbance, financial instability, or relationship disharmony—stems from imbalances in the Energy Body caused by accumulated negative karma.

Sittha Viruthi Yoga is designed to:

  • Cleanse negative karmic blocks
  • Activate the Energy Centers (Chakras)
  • Strengthen physical, mental, emotional & spiritual systems
  • Guide you into a harmonious, purposeful way of living
  • Offer simple practices with lifelong transformative results

These workshops are accessible to everyone, regardless of background or previous yogic experience.

The Four Levels of Sittha Viruthi Yoga

Each workshop is one day, followed by guided ongoing practice and support through the app.

1. Narkarma Viruthi

The Foundation of Health & Vitality

Good health is the seat of peace, joy, and higher awareness. The first level focuses on:

  • Cleansing karmic impressions affecting the physical body
  • Enhancing vitality through Energy-Body alignment
  • Improving wellness of body, mind, and relationships

This level lays the groundwork for all further transformation.

Learn More Narkarma Viruthi

2. Suya Viruthi

Mental Wellness & Harmonious Relationships

Once health stabilizes, the second level deepens the inner journey:

  • Techniques for emotional stability
  • Improved clarity and mental peace
  • Healing relationship karma
  • Practices for inner confidence and self-renewal

Entry to Level 2 requires consistent practice of Level 1 techniques and observed improvements.

Learn More Suya Viruthi

3. Yoga Viruthi – Part One

Prosperity, Abundance & Life Progress

The third level focuses on reshaping karmas related to wealth and growth.

  • Advanced meditations for prosperity
  • Clearing karmic blocks related to money & career
  • Sowing the seeds for sustained abundance
  • Revisiting health, mind & relationships for integrated progress

This program transforms your relationship with success.

Learn More Yoga Viruthi – Part One

4. Yoga Viruthi – Part Two

The Grammar of Life – Understanding Existence

This advanced level opens the gateway to the inner science of being.

  • In-depth exploration of Patanjali Yoga Sutras
  • Study of the layers of human consciousness
  • Awareness of life’s subtle laws
  • Mastery of the practices taught in all previous levels

Entry is granted only to dedicated practitioners who have cultivated a deep understanding through consistent practice.
