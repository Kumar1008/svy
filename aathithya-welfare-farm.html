Aathithya Welfare Farm – Nurturing Health Through Indian Traditional Wisdom

The Strength of Indian Traditional Medicine

Indian Traditional Medicine—including Siddha, Ayurveda, and Yoga—is built on the principles of natural healing, preventive care, and the harmony of body, mind, and spirit.

Key Benefits

  • Holistic Approach: Focuses on the individual as a whole—physical, mental, emotional, and spiritual well-being.
  • Natural Remedies: Utilizes herbs, minerals, diet, and lifestyle-based therapies passed down through generations.
  • Preventive Care: Encourages immunity, vitality, and long-term health rather than reacting only to disease.
  • Personalised Treatments: Remedies and routines are tailored to each person’s constitution (dosha) or body type.
  • Minimal Side Effects: When used appropriately under trained practitioners, traditional formulations are generally considered gentle and supportive.

Swarna Prashanam – Ancient Ayurvedic Support for Children’s Well-Being

Swarna Prashanam is a classical Ayurvedic preparation traditionally administered to children to support overall health. At Aathithya Welfare Farm, it is offered free of cost to the community every month, especially on Poosam (Pushya) Nakshatra, which is traditionally considered auspicious.

What is Swarna Prashanam?

It is an Ayurvedic formulation containing minute quantities of processed gold (swarna bhasma), herbal ghee, honey, and selected medicinal herbs. It has been described in classical Ayurvedic texts as supportive of children’s growth and development.

Traditionally Believed Benefits

(These benefits are based on traditional Ayurvedic perspectives; scientific evidence is still evolving.)

  • Supports the development of immunity and general wellness.
  • Encourages healthy digestion and metabolism.
  • Traditionally believed to enhance memory, focus, and cognitive functions.
  • Helps promote overall vitality and strength.
  • Considered beneficial for general growth and development in children.

Aathithya’s Free Monthly Swarna Prashanam Camp

This initiative ensures that families from all backgrounds can access traditional care for their children. The camp is open to the public, guided by professional Ayurvedic doctors.

Kalikkam (Siddha Eye Care)

A Free Monthly Siddha Eye Care Camp

Kalikkam—also known as Kallikka or Kalli—is a traditional Siddha eye formulation used for centuries as part of natural eye-care practices.

What is Kalikkam?

It is typically a blend of herbal ingredients such as Triphala, Amalaki, Haritaki, and other botanicals, sometimes mixed with rosewater or honey depending on the traditional method followed.

Traditionally Believed Benefits

  • Helps in soothing the eyes and reducing discomfort.
  • Used traditionally to ease eye strain caused by reading or prolonged screen time.
  • Long used as supportive care for minor eye irritations.
  • Known in Siddha tradition for its cooling and calming effect on the eyes.

Aathithya Welfare Farm offers Kalikkam as a free service to all, as a gesture of community wellness.

Foot Reflexology – Wellness Through the Feet

Foot Reflexology is a holistic practice where gentle pressure is applied to specific points on the feet believed to correspond to organs and systems in the body.

Potential Benefits

These reflect widely reported wellness benefits; effects vary from person to person.

  • Supports relaxation and helps reduce everyday stress
  • Promotes better circulation
  • Helps ease muscle tension and general discomfort
  • Supports healthy digestion
  • May improve overall vitality and energy flow

Reflexology sessions at Aathithya Welfare Farm provide a rejuvenating, calming experience for visitors seeking natural methods of wellness.
