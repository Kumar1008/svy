Anahata Chakra – The Fourth Chakra (Heart Chakra)

  • 12th February 2026

Just as the Sun holds a central and commanding role in the solar system, sustaining life, providing energy, and maintaining balance among the planets, the Manipuraga (Manipura) Chakra functions as the solar center of the human energy system. Among the seven Chakras, Manipuraga stands as the primary source of vitality, power, heat, and transformation. It radiates Prāṇa throughout the body, governs digestion and metabolism, and fuels willpower, confidence, and purposeful action. Without the Sun, the solar system collapses into darkness; likewise, without a balanced Manipuraga Chakra, the human system loses strength, clarity, and inner direction.

Meaning & Symbolism

  • Sanskrit Name: Manipura Manipuraga
  • Meaning: “City of Jewels” or “Resplendent Gem”
  • Element: Fire (Agni Tattva)
  • Color: Yellow / Golden
  • Symbol: Ten-petaled lotus with a downward-pointing triangle
  • Bīja Mantra: RAM

The Manipuraga Chakra represents the inner fire — the source of digestion, willpower, courage, and self-mastery.

Location

The Manipuraga Chakra is located:

  • In the navel region (Nābhi Sthānam)
  • Slightly above the navel, along the solar plexus
  • In the subtle spinal channel (Sushumna Nādi) at the level of the navel center

In Siddha and yogic physiology, this region is the seat of digestive fire (Jatharagni) and Pranic distribution.

Primary Functions & Importance

Center of Power and Will

Manipuraga Chakra governs:

  • Personal power
  • Self-confidence
  • Decision-making ability
  • Discipline and determination

A balanced Manipura Chakra enables a person to act with clarity, courage, and inner authority, without dominance or fear.

Digestive and Metabolic Control

This Chakra directly influences:

  • Stomach
  • Liver
  • Pancreas
  • Small intestine
  • Digestive enzymes and metabolism

Strong Manipura energy ensures:

  • Proper digestion
  • Efficient nutrient absorption
  • Balanced body heat and vitality

In Siddha philosophy, weakened Manipura leads to poor Agni, resulting in disease.

Transformation of Energy

Manipuraga Chakra acts as a transformational furnace:

  • Converts food into energy
  • Converts emotions into action
  • Converts experiences into wisdom

It bridges the lower instinctive Chakras and the higher spiritual Chakras, making it a vital center for spiritual evolution.

Emotional & Psychological Role

When balanced:

  • Confidence without arrogance
  • Calm leadership
  • Healthy ambition
  • Emotional stability

When imbalanced:

  • Anger, frustration, jealousy
  • Low self-esteem or excessive ego
  • Fear of rejection or failure
  • Digestive disorders linked to stress

Manipura Chakra in Siddha & Yoga Practice

In Sittha Viruthi Yoga, awakening the Manipuraga Chakra:

  • Strengthens Prāṇa Shakti
  • Purifies internal fire (Agni Shuddhi)
  • Prepares the seeker for higher meditative states
  • Stabilizes mind and body before Kundalini ascent

Practices such as:

  • Prāṇāyāma
  • Bandhas (especially Uddiyana Bandha)
  • Dhyāna on Agni Tattva
  • Mantra repetition (RAM)

help activate and balance this Chakra.

Spiritual Significance The Manipuraga Chakra teaches the seeker: Mastery over self precedes mastery over the world. It is the seat of inner sovereignty, where one learns to stand firmly in truth, action, and purpose.
