Yoga Viruthi – The Third Level of Sittha Viruthi Yoga

A Journey from Mastery of the Self to Mastery of Life

The third level—Yoga Viruthi—is a powerful expansion of the first two levels, Nara Karma Viruthi and Suya Viruthi. Only those who have practiced the earlier levels with sincerity and consistency become eligible to enter this transformative domain.

Yoga Viruthi is offered in two profound parts, each designed to elevate the practitioner’s physical vitality, mental clarity, spiritual depth, and life prosperity.

Yoga Viruthi – Part One

Prosperity, Planetary Harmony & Meditations for Life

Part One of Yoga Viruthi brings the aspirant into the world of prosperity consciousness, where the mind becomes the master key.

As the Bhagavad Gita reminds us:

“As the mind, so the person becomes.” (Gita 6.5)

The first two levels of Sittha Viruthi Yoga focus on health, relationships, and mental balance. Part One builds on this foundation by helping the practitioner understand the true nature of prosperity, including financial well-being, abundance, and life fulfillment.

Key Areas of Transformation

  • Meditations for as many needs of life
  • Meditation on the Five Elements (Pancha Bhootha)
  • Meditation on Nine Planets
  • Meditation on the Five Human Senses
  • Understanding the Incredible Empty Space—The Infinite Field of Consciousness

The universe is a vast ocean of omniscient, omnipotent, and omnipresent energy.The Mundaka Upanishad says:

“From the Infinite Brahman, the universe arises; in Him it lives; into Him it returns.”

This cosmic energy—expressed through space, elements, planets, and senses—is the powerhouse of all creation. Yoga Viruthi Part One teaches how to align with these mighty forces so the practitioner may walk the path of love, peace, joy, and prosperity.

Yoga Viruthi – Part Two

Grammar of Life: Human Creation, Consciousness & Patanjali Yoga Sutras

Part Two takes the practitioner deeper—into the core of human creation and the subtle layers of existence. Here, the seeker studies the three bodies:

  • The physical body
  • The energy (pranic) body
  • The astral and deeper layers of consciousness

This stage is grounded in the wisdom of Patanjali Yoga Sutras, the timeless grammar of human experience and yogic understanding.

As Patanjali declares:

“Yogaḥ citta-vṛtti-nirodhaḥ” Yoga is the stilling of the fluctuations of the mind.

In Part Two, Practitioners Learn:

  • The mechanics of human consciousness
  • How thought, energy, and awareness shape our reality
  • Yogic principles that govern spiritual evolution
  • Higher practices that awaken clarity, inner strength, and supreme balance

To enter this level, one must have consistent practice, a steady mind, and deep understanding.

The Transformative Path Continues

Upon completion, practitioners receive ongoing updation classes that refine:

  • Asanas
  • Pranayama
  • Strengthening and stretching practices
  • Meditative techniques

These support continuous transformation and harmony in all dimensions of life.

Sittha Viruthi Yoga – A Complete, Practical & Soulful Way of Life

Life is meant to be experienced in its full radiance. The Bhagavad Gita reminds us:

“One who is harmonious in mind, word, and action attains peace.” (Gita 5.11)

Sittha Viruthi Yoga guides the practitioner into such harmony—where health flourishes, relationships bloom, the mind becomes serene, and life’s purpose becomes clear. t is not merely a yoga system— It is a pathway to a life of awareness, abundance, compassion, and inner mastery.
