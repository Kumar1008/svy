A Structured Path to Energy Mastery, Inner Balance & Spiritual Evolution

Course Overview

The Healing Course is designed to equip students with deep knowledge and practical skills in Siddha–Yogic energy healing, covering physical, emotional, mental, and astral dimensions. The course integrates ancient wisdom from the Vedas, Siddha Agamas, Thirumoolar’s Thirumandiram, and Agasthiar teachings, combined with practical healing methods suitable for modern life.
