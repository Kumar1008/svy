About Sittha Viruthi Yoga

When the mind becomes still, the soul speaks.

Sittha Viruthi Yoga is a unique and transformative yogic school of thought founded in June 2021 by Mr. Vivekanandan, an intuitive seeker, healer, and devotee of ancient spiritual wisdom. Born from his personal journey through healing, inner revelation, and the pursuit of human upliftment, Sittha Viruthi Yoga stands today as a beacon of knowledge, compassion, and conscious living.

The Genesis of Sittha Viruthi Yoga

The roots of Sittha Viruthi Yoga trace back to the deep spiritual inclinations of its founder. Mr. Vivekanandan has been profoundly inspired by the teachings of Vethathiri Maharishi, the Siddha traditions, and the advanced healing system for which he is a certified higher-level healer.

His passion for Traditional Indian Medicine—Siddha and Ayurveda—led him into extensive research of their healing properties. This vision resulted in the establishment of Aathithya Herbal, a manufacturing unit dedicated to producing pure Siddha and Ayurvedic medicines based entirely on ancient scripts and formulations. These products are prepared with utmost authenticity and have received certification from the Ministry of AYUSH, Government of India.

A Turning Point:
Healing Through Inner Science

While his spiritual research and healing practices were growing, destiny placed him at a crossroads. During a flight journey in Delhi, he suffered a severe heart ailment requiring emergency medical intervention. Doctors advised prolonged rest and medication. Yet, at this crucial moment, the real inner transformation began.

Through deep meditation, chakra cleansing, and the application of yogic and spiritual science he had studied, he examined his own body’s physical and astral mechanisms. With unwavering discipline, he applied advanced energy practices on himself. To the surprise of medical experts, his heart condition gradually improved, and he regained complete, robust health.

This miraculous self-recovery became the seed of a profound realization— “Knowledge practiced becomes transformation; transformation shared becomes service.”

Birth of a Yogic Vision

Empowered by the blessings of masters like Thirumoolar, Agasthiar, Patanjali, Vethathiri Maharishi, Master Luong Minh Dang and other Siddhars, Mr. Vivekanandan recognized his soul purpose: to impart to humanity the spiritual science that had transformed his own life.

Thus emerged Sittha Viruthi Yoga, a holistic system built upon the pillars of traditional yogic wisdom, energy science, and practical life transformation. June 2021 became a milestone—the sacred beginning of a new spiritual movement.

Aathithya Welfare Farm

Nurturing Health Through Indian Traditional Wisdom

Aathithya Welfare Farm, a branch of Siththa Viruthi Yoga, is dedicated to taking the profound wisdom of Indian Traditional Medicine—Siddha and Ayurveda—to people from all walks of life. Guided by experienced Siddha and Ayurvedic doctors, the centre offers holistic treatments rooted in age-old healing systems that focus on balance, prevention, and total well-being.
